# Vectordelia asset credits

These vector-style images come from the **Frutiger Aero Archive** resource page:

- https://frutigeraeroarchive.org/resources/metro

Downloaded on 2026-11-09, then resized and converted to WebP with transparency for this
personal, non-commercial portfolio. The archive describes the collection as images for
making Frutiger Aero-themed collages, images and edits.

background keyed out. `scene-top.webp` is the original image with the white background
keyed out. Both are split into vertical layers for the entrance animation.
