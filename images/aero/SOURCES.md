# Aero asset sources and licence notes

All archive imagery in this folder was downloaded from
[Frutiger Aero Archive](https://frutigeraeroarchive.org/) on 2026-10-24 and converted
locally to WebP. The archive states that its images are preserved for archival,
educational and personal use, and that **no commercial use is permitted** unless
stated otherwise. Several originals come from Asadal.com or other unnamed sources.

This portfolio is currently personal and non-commercial. Replace or properly licence
these images before any commercial use.

## Processing

- Converted with Pillow 12.3 to WebP.
- Wallpapers resized to 2400 × 1350 and saved at quality 82.
- Transparent PNGs trimmed to their alpha bounds, resized where useful, and saved
  with alpha at quality 85–88.
- No crop, compositing or generative editing was applied to the source imagery.
- No Microsoft logos, operating-system wallpapers or trademarked marks are included.
